For teachers: how to use this resource
  • Share the link. Each student fills in their own book on a phone or computer; nobody sees anyone else’s answers.
  • Use it in one full class (45–90 min) or one page per class: progress stays saved on the same device.
  • For the signatures page, give students 10 minutes to swap devices and write each other messages.
  • At the end, each student downloads their book and prints it or saves it as a PDF.
